Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

The interface is available in five languages. This legal-page body is currently provided as English / Traditional Chinese reference text.

Last updated: July 13, 2026.

Local image processing

Images selected in BeadsPixel are processed only in your browser. The website does not upload image files or generated patterns to our servers, and it does not provide an image-storage service. The editor uses temporary browser object URLs for previews and releases them when a new image is selected or the page is closed.

Data we store

The site attempts to store only your selected interface language in your browser’s localStorage under bead-lang. If browser storage is unavailable, the site continues without saving that preference. We do not use analytics, advertising tags, tracking pixels, or tracking cookies.

Third-party requests

The pages load the Nunito font from Google Fonts. The editor loads the jsPDF library from cdnjs when the editor page opens; it is not deferred until you click PDF export. Those providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and the requested resource under their own privacy policies. Your selected image is not sent to either provider by BeadsPixel.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hokidev26@gmail.com.
