Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

Data Collection

This tool does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All image processing is performed entirely within your browser using client-side JavaScript.

Images

Any images you upload are processed locally in your browser and are never sent to any server. No images are stored, cached, or shared.

Cookies & Local Storage

We only use localStorage to save your language preference. No tracking cookies are used.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Fonts — used to load the Nunito typeface
  • cdnjs — used to load the jsPDF library for PDF generation

These services may collect basic usage data according to their own privacy policies.

Analytics

This tool does not use any analytics or tracking services.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at hokidev26@gmail.com.
